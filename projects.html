Operating since late 2023

The Okahandja farm

An aquaponic greenhouse and a soil-based herb net house outside Okahandja, 9,800 m² under cover and one of the largest aquaponics facilities in Africa. It supplies lettuce and herbs to Namibian retailers all year, on off-grid solar.

In development

Potato processing, Namibia

Namibia imports all of its frozen French fries, about 4,800 tonnes a year. Oribi is developing a processing plant to make them locally, supplied increasingly by Namibian growers. The feasibility work and financial model are complete, and we are working with the regulator, growers, equipment suppliers and funders. We are now speaking to strategic and financial investors. Detail is shared under a non-disclosure agreement.

Import figure: Oribi estimate based on Namibian Agronomic Board data, 2023/24.
